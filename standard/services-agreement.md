# Fractional CPO Services Agreement · Open Template v1.0
### Part of the Fractional Rates Index engagement standard · CC BY 4.0
### This is a working template, not legal advice. Have a lawyer adapt §7-§11 to your jurisdiction.

## 1. Parties and Engagement
1.1 This agreement is between **[Company legal name]** ("Company") and
**[Executive legal name / entity]** ("Executive"), effective **[date]**.
1.2 Engagement structure: the Executive serves as **Fractional Chief
Product Officer**, an independent contractor providing part-time,
ongoing product leadership. Nothing here creates employment, agency, or
exclusivity; the Executive maintains other clients, and the Company
acknowledges this is inherent to the fractional model.

## 2. Fees and Payment
2.1 Monthly retainer: **$[amount]** for **[20-25] hours per month**,
delivered per the working pattern in §3.
2.2 Payment terms: invoiced **at the start of each month, payable within
[7] days**. The first invoice covers the minimum term's first month and
is a condition of start.
2.3 Fee review: rates hold for the initial term; afterward either party
may propose an adjustment with **[30] days** notice, applying from the
next renewal.
2.4 Expenses: pre-approved, at cost, invoiced monthly. Travel beyond
**[city/remote norm]** requires written approval.

## 3. Time, Availability and Overage
3.1 Working pattern: a fixed weekly leadership session plus asynchronous
availability with a **[24-48 hour]** response norm on business days.
3.2 The monthly hour band is a good-faith planning envelope, not a
meter; single months may vary **[±20%]** without adjustment.
3.3 Sustained overage (two consecutive months above the band) triggers a
scope conversation, not silent invoicing.

## 4. Scope of Services
4.1 The Executive owns, at leadership altitude: product strategy and its
exclusions, prioritization decisions within the agreed strategy, the
product decision process, customer-evidence direction, and coaching of
the internal product team.
4.2 The Executive advises on (Company decides): hiring and compensation,
pricing, fundraising narrative, and changes to the strategy itself.
4.3 Out of scope: day-to-day execution (tickets, specs, ceremonies)
beyond coaching; people-management authority unless separately agreed.
4.4 The detailed 90-day outcomes, decision rights and deliverables live
in the attached **Scope of Work**, which controls where more specific.

## 5. Term and Termination
5.1 Initial term: **[3] months** minimum, then month-to-month.
5.2 Notice: after the initial term, either party may end with **[30]
days** written notice, effective end of the notice month.
5.3 Immediate termination: material breach uncured within **[14]** days
of written notice; non-payment beyond **[14]** days past due; or conduct
creating legal or reputational risk. Fees earned to date remain due.
5.4 On any ending, §6 (IP), §7 (Confidentiality) and §9 (Liability)
survive.

## 6. Intellectual Property
6.1 Work product created specifically for the Company under this
agreement is assigned to the Company on payment.
6.2 The Executive retains pre-existing materials, methods, frameworks
and templates; the Company receives a perpetual, non-exclusive license
to those as embedded in delivered work.
6.3 Anonymized learnings and patterns may inform the Executive's future
work and publications, subject to §7.

## 7. Confidentiality
7.1 Each party protects the other's non-public information with at
least the care it applies to its own, and uses it only for this
engagement.
7.2 Exclusions: information public through no fault, independently
developed, or lawfully received elsewhere.
7.3 Survives **[3] years** beyond termination. Customer data handling
complies with applicable data-protection law; the Executive accesses
personal data only as needed and under the Company's policies.

## 8. Conflicts and Non-Solicitation
8.1 The Executive will not simultaneously serve a **direct competitor**
(defined: **[definition scoped to segment/ICP, not the whole
industry]**) without written disclosure and consent.
8.2 Neither party solicits the other's employees or contractors during
the engagement and for **[6] months** after.

## 9. Liability and Insurance
9.1 Aggregate liability of either party is capped at fees paid or
payable in the **[3] months** preceding the claim; neither party is
liable for indirect or consequential damages.
9.2 Nothing limits liability for fraud, willful misconduct, or breaches
of §6-§7.
9.3 The Executive warrants professional-grade care; the Company
acknowledges outcomes depend on its own execution and market factors.

## 10. Handover
10.1 Fractional done well ends deliberately: on wind-down, the Executive
delivers the documented strategy, the running decision process, open
decision log, and, where relevant, the hiring profile for a successor,
within the final month's hours.
10.2 A **[2-4 hour]** transition session with the successor or founder
is included in the final month.

## 11. General
11.1 Governing law: **[jurisdiction]**; disputes first to good-faith
executive discussion, then **[mediation/arbitration/courts]**.
11.2 Entire agreement; amendments in writing. Neither party may assign
without consent, except the Executive's assignment to their own service
entity.
11.3 Notices: email to the addresses on the signature block, deemed
received next business day.
